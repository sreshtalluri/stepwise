# Name candidates for the public launch

Working name stays `stepwise` through the cohort. Pick before week 6. Check .com/.app, App Store, Instagram/TikTok handle, and trademark before falling in love. Domain/handle availability below is **unchecked** — that's the homework.

## The angle: "the fancam you can't find" / see the dance from anywhere

| Name | Why | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Fancam** | Exactly the job; dancers already use the word for "the angle I wish I had" | Generic K-pop term; handles surely taken; trademark risk zero but discoverability low |
| **Anyangle** | Says the feature; easy to spell and say | Bland; .com likely taken |
| **Orbit** | The gesture (orbit the dancer); one word, warm | Extremely common product name |
| **Sidestep** | Dance word + "see it from the side"; playful | A tax/legal term too; fine |
| **Turnaround** | Dance and animation term (character turnaround sheet = seeing the body from every angle) — a genuine insider double meaning | Slightly corporate ("business turnaround") |
| **Backview** | The most-requested angle, literally | Descriptive, hard to own |

## The angle: learning / counts / steps

| Name | Why | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Eightcount** / **8count** | Every dancer counts in eights; instantly "for dancers" | Others use it (studios, apps) — check hard |
| **Andfive** ("5, 6, 7, 8") | The universal dance count-in; sounds like a brand | Explaining it to non-dancers takes a second |
| **Stepwise** (keep) | Already yours; means "one step at a time" | Collides with a stats method, a YC company, and a notation term |
| **Marking** | Dancers "mark" choreography (walk through it small before full-out) — the exact learning behavior the app supports | Insider-only; sounds like grading to outsiders |
| **Fullout** | The opposite: dancing at full energy; aspirational | Two words, spelling ambiguity |

## The angle: the body / the mesh / the ghost

| Name | Why | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghost** / **Ghostdance** | The repo's own "ghost overlay"; a translucent body you follow | "Ghost Dance" has a specific historical/religious meaning — avoid |
| **Mirrorly** | Mirror mode is the default follow-along view; friendly | -ly suffix is dated |
| **Bodyline** | Dance term (the line of the body) + the skeleton line | Also a bodybuilding/fitness term |
| **Silhouette** | What you see: the clean body, no face | Long; hard to spell for handles |

## Short, ownable, abstract (highest chance of a clean domain)

| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **Vantage** | An angle you couldn't get; a point of view — the whole pitch in one word |
| **Kinesis** | Movement; clean, techy |
| **Choreo** | Dancers' shorthand for choreography; friendly. Likely taken (choreo.com), check |
| **Tandava** | Shiva's cosmic dance — a nod to the community you're launching in; distinctive, ownable, slightly heavy for a TikTok-trend app |
| **Lasya** | The graceful counterpart to tandava; short, soft, likely available |
| **Adavu** | Bharatanatyam's basic step unit — literally "the steps"; very ownable, needs explaining outside the community |

## My short list

1. **Vantage** — the pitch in one word, works for casual and serious dancers, likely ownable with a modifier (vantage.dance, getvantage).
2. **Turnaround** — real double meaning for dancers and animators; memorable.
3. **Andfive** — fun, unmistakably dance, sounds like something friends would say.
4. **Lasya** — if you want the community nod without the weight of Tandava.
5. **Stepwise** — the fallback if nothing else clears.

Whatever you pick: the `.dance` TLD exists and is cheap, and it reads well ("vantage.dance", "andfive.dance") if the .com is gone.
